JARVIS ASSISTANT
================

TO START
  Click  Start-Jarvis-Max.bat  once. Jarvis's window opens by itself.

  A black window also opens. That's Jarvis's engine. Leave it open; closing it
  switches Jarvis off. If you close only Jarvis's window, clicking the .bat again
  just reopens it.

THE FIRST START IS DIFFERENT (one time only)
  1. It downloads Jarvis's parts, about 1 GB in total. That takes a few minutes.
  2. A Claude page opens in your browser. Sign in and click Authorize.
     This connects Jarvis's brain (Claude Code) to your Claude plan. There is
     no API key and no extra cost. Jarvis never sees your password.
  3. The first time you press TALK or CAMERA, the window asks to use the
     microphone or camera. Click Allow.
  4. The chip at the top says EARS LOADING for the first minute or two while
     the speech model downloads. You can type to Jarvis in the meantime.

THE BUTTONS
  TALK      Click, speak, click again (it says SEND IT) and Jarvis answers aloud.
            Keyboard: F2 does the same.
  Type box  Type and press Enter, or click SEND.
  CAMERA    Turns the webcam on or off. While it's on, Jarvis sees what the
            camera sees with every question. Jarvis can also take a picture
            by itself when you ask, "what am I holding?"
  SCREEN    Jarvis looks at your screen. Type a question first, or just click.
  SEARCH    Searches the web for what's in the box.
  MEMORY    What Jarvis has saved about you. Click a note to read it.
  HANDS     ON = Jarvis may use your mouse and keyboard. OFF = look, don't touch.
  VOICE     Spoken replies on or off.
  LOG       The whole conversation. There's a button at the bottom to start fresh.
  BRAIN     Pick Jarvis's brain: Claude Sonnet, Opus or Haiku (quickest), Gemini,
            Gemini Flash-Lite (quickest Gemini, same key), Local Qwen (on this
            PC) or Auto (Claude first, local when your Claude limit runs out).
            Jarvis restarts by itself (about 15 seconds) and then says which
            brain really came up. You can also just say "switch to Opus".
            Gemini asks once for a Gemini API key (free at aistudio.google.com/apikey);
            it's kept in the secrets folder and never goes to GitHub.
  FACE      Switches between Jarvis four faces: Circuit Board, Radial,
            Face in the Code and Neural Core.
  THUMBS    Thumbs up = that answer was right. Thumbs down = wrong (type what was
            wrong in the box first if you like). Saying "no, I meant..." or
            "wrong" counts as thumbs down; "perfect" or "well done" as thumbs up.
  STOP      Stops Jarvis at once, mid-sentence or mid-task. Keyboard: Esc.

  The four chips at the top say whether BRAIN, EARS, VOICE and HANDS are ready.

EMERGENCY STOP FOR THE HANDS
  Push the mouse into any corner of the screen. That halts any mouse or keyboard
  action Jarvis is doing.

LOCAL BRAIN (Qwen, on this PC)
  Jarvis has a second brain that runs on your own laptop through Ollama. It can
  still go online: research, web search, reading pages and your multi search, plus
  the screen, camera, mouse, keyboard, files and PowerShell (commands ask ALLOW).
  The BRAIN chip says LOCAL when it's answering.
  The BRAIN button picks the brain (it writes "brain_mode" in app\jarvis.json):
    "local"  always the local brain
    "auto"   Claude first; the local brain when your Claude limit runs out
             (for 30 minutes) or Claude can't be reached
    "claude" Claude only

JARVIS LEARNS EVERY NIGHT
  Every request is logged (logs\turns.jsonl) with your thumbs. Once a day, at
  night when you're not using him, the local Qwen brain (free) reviews the day:
    - lessons from what went wrong        -> memory\notes\Lessons.md
    - step-by-step skills from jobs that worked -> memory\notes\Skills.md
  Then it rewrites ALL old + new lessons and skills (and any memory note that
  changed) as one fresh, short bullet list: no repeats, no contradictions (the
  newest point wins). Originals are copied to memory\.growth\archive first.
  A test sheet of your real requests is run before and after; if the score
  drops, the old lessons and skills come back. Every brain reads the lessons.
  LOG panel > LEARN NOW does it straight away. Needs Ollama running.

GITHUB: BUILD AND PUBLISH
  Say "download and install github.com/<owner>/<project>". Jarvis shows you the
  project's stars and last update, downloads it to Desktop\Jarvis-Builds, and a
  stronger AI (Claude, or Gemini) writes a checklist (PLAN.md). Jarvis works through
  it one step at a time (ALLOW each), asks the stronger AI when a step fails, and
  stops after 3 tries on one step.
  Say "push <folder> to GitHub as <name>". Jarvis checks for keys and private files,
  writes a README if needed, shows you a preview (ALLOW), then pushes. For a new
  repo he opens GitHub's page; you click Create repository.

WHEN JARVIS ASKS PERMISSION
  Before Jarvis runs a command that changes something on your PC, or edits a file
  outside its memory, a box appears with ALLOW and DENY. Nothing happens until
  you click one. (Look-only commands, clicks, typing and opening apps don't ask;
  the HANDS button controls the mouse and keyboard.)

WHERE THINGS ARE
  memory\          Jarvis's memory (the vault). CLAUDE.md is its identity and
                   rules. Open any note in Notepad and edit it yourself.
  app\jarvis.json  Settings: name, voice, what it calls you.
  logs\jarvis.log  What happened, if something goes wrong.

IF SOMETHING'S WRONG
  - BRAIN says SIGN IN: click the SIGN IN button in the window.
  - EARS says ERROR: the speech model couldn't download. Check the internet
    and start Jarvis again. Typing still works.
  - VOICE says BACKUP: Microsoft's voice service couldn't be reached, so the
    window's built-in voice is speaking. It switches back by itself.
