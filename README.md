# Jarvis-Max

> 🆕 **New to AI or GitHub? Start with the hand-written [beginner's notebook](NEWBIES-START-HERE.md).**

A voice-first AI assistant for your Windows PC **and your Android phone**. Talk to it in English or Hinglish and it answers out loud. It sees your screen and webcam, uses your mouse and keyboard (and your phone's screen) when you allow it, researches the web, keeps a Markdown memory, learns from its mistakes every night, and **learns brand-new abilities when you ask it to**.

- **8 swappable brains:** Claude Sonnet, Opus and Haiku (your Claude plan), Gemini and Gemini Flash-Lite (free API key), DeepSeek V4 (API key), local Qwen through Ollama (free and private), or Auto. Switch from a menu or by voice.
- **Ears for Indian English and Hinglish:** Sarvam Saaras V4, with local faster-whisper (on your NVIDIA GPU when there is one) as an automatic backup. Hands-free listening keeps working when the window is minimised.
- **Eyes and hands on both devices:** screen, OCR, webcam, mouse, keyboard, apps and the web on the PC. On Android, the Jarvis-Hands™ app reads the screen, opens apps, taps, types, scrolls and makes calls after your ALLOW.
- **The Living Face:** a holographic face with 131 expressions, driven by what Jarvis is really doing, plus vitals (level, XP, morale, energy, confidence).
- **He grows:** 👍/👎 feedback becomes nightly lessons and skills (kept only if a test sheet scores the same or better). Lessons that last become wisdom. He runs a self-check of his own hands and proposes repairs. Say *"learn how to …"* and Claude Opus builds him a new ability for you to approve and test.
- **Safety first:** every risky step waits for your ALLOW. He never types passwords, PINs, OTPs or card numbers, never pays, and keys never go to GitHub.

**Quick start (Windows 10/11):** download the repo, double-click `Start-Jarvis-Max.bat`, sign in to Claude (or paste a free Gemini key), and say "hello".
